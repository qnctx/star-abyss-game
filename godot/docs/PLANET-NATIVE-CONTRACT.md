# Godot native planet integration contract

Status: integration contract for the native globe migration. The initial six-kilometre basin, investigation sites, camp, vehicle, Riftwing duel, inventory and save progression remain playable.

## One coordinate system

- The planet identity and radius are `star-abyss`, `star-abyss-planet-v1`, version `1`, and **120000 m**. This is the engineering radius in `docs/development/PLANET-ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- Authoritative saved positions are planet-centred Cartesian **double** coordinates in the browser planet convention: Y is the north pole; positive longitude faces `-Z`. `planet.position` is the player position in that convention. Never save a floating-origin rebased render coordinate as the authoritative planet position.
- The native scene is a fixed rigid transform of those coordinates. Its planet centre is `(0, -120000, 0)`, so the old basin tangent is `(0, 0, 0)` and local axes remain east `+X`, up `+Y`, south `+Z`. Convert a browser canonical point `p` to native scene position as `(-p.z, p.x - 120000, -p.y)`; inverse is `(scene.y + 120000, -scene.z, -scene.x)`. Thus an old basin `(x,y,z)` maps to canonical `(120000+y,-z,-x)` exactly. Legacy position values can continue to drive the basin while the planet field blends out by 4 km.
- All movement, collision, altitude and orientation outside the basin use radial up from the native planet centre and the same solid terrain field that renders the globe. Do not use `scene.y`, `height_at(x,z)`, or `abs(x/z) <= 3000` as a global altitude/boundary test. Ground projection is radial. Ocean surface is distinct from solid ground.
- A render floating origin, if used, is a presentation detail applied consistently to player, world, camp, enemies, vehicle, effects and collision. Render rebasing must never change saved canonical positions. Do not silently save a far scene position in an old local field.

## Native APIs to connect

The world and player implementations have agreed on these scene-space method signatures. `native_planet_coordinates.gd` owns the double-valued canonical dictionary conversion and geodetic helpers.

- `world.planet_enabled` identifies sphere mode. `world.surface_at(scene_position)` returns `{ready, point, normal, agl, water_depth, spacing}` for the solid radial surface; `world.up_at(scene_position)` returns radial up. An unready sample must not be interpreted as safe ground.
- `world.is_solid_at(scene_position, radius, height)` checks the same authored terrain/collision field as the visible globe; all hemispheres are valid. `world.sweep(from_scene, to_scene, radius, height)` returns `{ready, clear}`. `world.update_stream(scene_position)` accepts a native scene position. `surface_at().ready` requires rendered and collidable support at the radial footprint, including when the character is high above it; the original basin may use its existing terrain collider. Touchdown, teleport and vehicle exits wait for that real collider. An abstract field sample alone is insufficient for a safe landing.
- R6–R9 Q uses `world.prepare_route(from_scene, to_scene, radius, height)` for at most 2200 m. It pins both original-basin chunks and planet near tiles until actual path support is ready, then the ascension and player code still do segmented collision validation. `release_prepared_route()` runs after success, failure, cancellation or timeout. A pending cast costs no energy/cooldown and cancels on movement, turning, injury, state change, mounting or duel jump; it cannot commit unexpectedly from a stale input.

### R8/R9 radial landing and finite ground impact

- Long-range return to ground uses a separate `world.prepare_surface_landing(from_scene, max_distance, cast_id)` ticket. It streams only the destination footprint, checks a live dry terrain collider, slope and capsule clearance, and returns `ready/clear/ticket/target/surface/normal`. `world.consume_surface_landing(ticket, current_scene, body_radius, body_height)` checks the World-owned one-use ticket again immediately before teleport, including expiry, source pose, world-origin revision, terrain-deformation revision and live support. `world.cancel_surface_landing(ticket)` releases pins. The target is the physical support point plus radial up by 0.8 m; ordinary Q blink and its 2200 m route contract remain as above. A failed or pending landing does not move the player or charge the cast.
- A high-power ground contact may request one authoritative `surface_impact_requested` event from `NativeEnvironmentDamage` when `deform_surface` is explicit and power is at least 600. The first supported crater range is radius 6–12 m and depth 1–3 m. `NativeSurfaceDeformation` holds at most 64 sparse, non-overlapping, canonical unit-direction records, keyed by source/cast/phase. Its height profile is a smooth finite bowl with a small raised rim and zero displacement outside the radius. The module rejects duplicate IDs, overlapping locations and invalid or cross-planet saves; a proposal is not saved until the corresponding visible mesh and physical collider commit together.
- Both the legacy basin and globe must sample each committed offset exactly once from their original base height, rebuild affected triangles at metre-scale resolution, and keep the previous collider active until replacement is ready. Restoring `surface_impacts` from the main native save happens before player landing and terrain streaming. On successful visual and collision commit, World calls ecology `invalidate_surface_region(origin, radius)` so trees, rocks and creatures recheck the new support. Damage and shockwave results remain independent of whether terrain deformation is accepted.
- As of 2026-09-30, the canonical record module, World event/save bridge, one-use landing ticket, metre-scale basin/globe near mesh and triangle collision are connected. Each asynchronous impact is acknowledged only after the replacement collider is checked on a physics frame; a failed check restores the previous sparse snapshot. Isolated headless checks pass for a basin crater, a blend-apron crater, an outer-globe crater, stream return, save restore, 125 km R9 ground return, R8 range rejection, ticket cancellation and one-use consumption. From the repository root, run `D:\Godot_v4.6.2-stable_win64.exe\Godot_v4.6.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --path godot --script res://tests/native_surface_deformation_verify.gd`, then the same command with `native_surface_impact_integrated.gd` and `native_surface_impact_globe_verify.gd`. The far globe was rebaked with `res://scripts/native_planet_bake.gd`; `native_planet_far_cache_verify.gd` checks that the source manifest matches and all six runtime faces load at 192 segments. Full controller combat and art review remain separate acceptance steps.
- `world.origin_revision` and an origin shift signal permit the main scene to move all scene roots in the same physics frame if floating-origin rebasing is enabled. The canonical position remains unchanged.
- `player.teleport_to_surface(scene_position)` verifies a radial ground destination plus clearance and enters it; `player.blink_to(scene_position)` keeps safe-route semantics. Legacy `teleport_to_ground(x,z)` may remain for basin tests and F2/F3 conveniences only.
- Scene reset, vehicle exit and investigation use native scene positions; save and restore convert to and from canonical doubles at the boundary. Existing combat and VFX continue to use local scene vectors, with radial up for offsets on the globe.

## Save migration and protection

- Preserve the current root envelope (`version: 1`, `player`, `riftwing`, `enemy_r3`, `ascension`, `combat`, `skimmer`, `fragments`, `investigated`). Add an optional `planet` object with `version`, `id`, `seed`, `radius`, and canonical `position`; include `forward`, `velocity`, canonical `skimmer`, and `skimmer_forward`. The existing enemies remain basin-bound and keep their current root snapshots. Do not reset story, inventory, loot-consumed state or old investigation IDs during migration.
- For a save without `planet`, interpret old `player.x/y/z` as basin tangent coordinates and map it to canonical with the transform above. Likewise map old `skimmer` and enemy positions. Do this once on read; write an additive `planet` envelope on next save. Invalid planet identity/coordinates must produce a recoverable error or explicit migration, never silent clamp to the basin.
- When the player is on the globe outside the legacy basin, retain a safe legacy basin pose and zero legacy velocity fields in root `player` for old-version compatibility. Keep health, energy, realm, camera state and `flight_active`; record actual position, direction and velocity in `planet`. On restore, `planet` position and velocity win. Mounted saves use the validated exit pose and zero actual velocity for a safe dismount on reload.
- The existing all-zero teardown repair applies **only** when `planet` is absent and the player, skimmer and Riftwing legacy positions all carry that exact early-build signature. The overlap recovery probes around the current radial tangent and must not fall back to camp merely because X/Z exceeds 3 km. Back up before modifying a damaged save.
- If a valid planet position cannot obtain a collidable destination within 600 physics frames, keep the original file read-only and enter at the saved legacy safe pose with a clear warning. Never autosave that fallback over the planet position.
- Tests must use isolated save slots. Verify an old basin save, a migrated near-basin save, and saved positions beyond 3 km, near a pole and on the back hemisphere through save/reload without coordinate clamping. Verify inventory, investigated IDs and enemy loot state in the same reload.

## Gameplay placement and diagnostics

- The camp, sites, Riftwing arena and skimmer start remain at their old basin tangent coordinates. F2/F3 intentionally return to the established arena; F3 resets training enemies but must not replenish already-consumed loot. A long-distance return to the basin is an explicit action, never a restore side effect.
- The skimmer steers about radial up, projects wheel/body support radially and checks loaded collidable terrain before movement. Dismount uses a local tangent search and commits only when the player can stand there. On mounting, camera up and vehicle body orientation follow radial up. If vehicle driving is deliberately basin-only for this slice, display that limit clearly and keep remote planet travel on foot/flight; do not let a planar car cross to an unsupported area.
- HUD states the planet scope and displays radial altitude. Investigation guidance uses actual scene distance to existing basin sites and a local tangent bearing. Chinese text must remain readable and buttons visible/clickable at 960×540 and normal desktop sizes.
- Verification distinguishes physics frames from mocked stepping and independently confirms a *visible and collidable* globe at low and high altitude. Existing basin mobility/combat/vehicle/save loops must be rerun after connection.
