# Scanner concept generation

Tool: built-in image_gen. Generated design is a reference for local Blender modelling, not a screenshot of the shipped 3D asset.

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a polished game-production concept sheet for a first-person exploration scanner and its equip/use/stow animation, matching a realistic C2 explorer suit: charcoal-brown woven fabric gloves and sleeves, aged ivory mineral-shell forearm plates with fine gold seams, black joints, restrained amber accents. Landscape sheet on warm grey studio background, no logos. Top row: large precise product reference of ONE palm-sized handheld scanner in front, side and rear views; short ergonomic pistol-free grip below a rounded angular ivory shell, recessed dark glass display with a subtle cyan circular radar, small amber side status lights, two compact folding sensor fins. Manufacturable cohesive object, not a gun or a phone, no giant holograms. Bottom row: FIVE consistent first-person storyboard panels with same camera and same scanner, labels exactly 'EMPTY', 'DRAW', 'SCAN', 'FOLD', 'STOW'. EMPTY shows unobstructed horizon and no item; DRAW right gloved hand lifts the device from lower-right edge; SCAN device held low-right with clear center vision, left index finger briefly touches the display; FOLD sensor fins close as hands lower; STOW scanner and both hands disappear below frame. Anatomically correct gloved hands with five fingers. Crisp readable silhouettes, believable materials and joints, neutral lighting, production concept art not a screenshot. Keep prop geometry identical across views and panels. The essential design is normal exploration empty-handed, a deliberate brief draw then scan then fold and stow.
